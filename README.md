# 🔬 Interactive 3D Science & Simulation Lab

**A stunning educational platform for exploring science through immersive 3D visualizations**

[🚀 Live Demo](https://rudra496.github.io/science/) | [📦 GitHub](https://github.com/rudra496/science)

---

## ✨ Features

### ⚛️ 3D Periodic Table
- ✅ All **118 elements** with complete data
- ✅ Interactive element tiles with hover effects
- ✅ Search and filter by category
- ✅ Full element details (mass, electron config, uses, facts)
- ✅ **OrbitControls** - drag to rotate, scroll to zoom

### 🪐 Solar System Explorer
- ✅ All **8 planets** with realistic orbits
- ✅ Saturn's rings, moons, orbit trails
- ✅ Adjustable orbit speed and planet scale
- ✅ **Full 3D rotation and zoom controls**
- ✅ Sun with glowing corona effect

### 🧬 DNA/RNA Visualizer
- ✅ Animated **double helix** with base pairs
- ✅ Color-coded nucleotides (A, T, G, C)
- ✅ Adjustable helix turns and rotation speed
- ✅ **Interactive camera controls**

### 🔬 Cell Biology Lab
- ✅ **Animal cell** with organelles (nucleus, mitochondria, ER, ribosomes)
- ✅ Detailed 3D structure
- ✅ **Rotate and zoom** to explore
- ✅ Smooth animations

### 💡 Physics Lab
- ✅ **Young's Double Slit** experiment with particle visualization
- ✅ Wave interference patterns
- ✅ **Play/Pause/Reset** controls
- ✅ Adjustable simulation speed

### 🤖 Robotics Academy
- ✅ **6-DOF Robotic Arm** with joints
- ✅ **Individual joint controls** (base, shoulder, elbow, wrist, gripper)
- ✅ **Auto Demo mode** - watch the arm move
- ✅ **Full rotation and zoom**

### 🎮 Space Survival Game
- ✅ 3D space combat with enemy waves
- ✅ **D-pad and WASD controls**
- ✅ **Fire button** and keyboard shooting
- ✅ Score, lives, and wave tracking
- ✅ Multiple difficulty levels

---

## 🎮 Controls

| Scene | Action |
|-------|--------|
| **3D Scenes** | Left-drag to rotate, Scroll to zoom, Right-drag to pan |
| **Periodic Table** | Click elements for details, Use search and filters |
| **Solar System** | Adjust sliders for speed and scale |
| **Game** | WASD/Arrows to move, Space to shoot |

## 🚀 Deploy to GitHub Pages

1. **Create repository**: `rudra496/science`
2. **Upload files**:
   - `index.html`
   - `style.css`
   - `script.js`
3. **Enable GitHub Pages**: Settings → Pages → Source: `main` branch
4. **Done!** Live at: `https://rudra496.github.io/science/`
